Privacy policy

Your journal

Medic Logger uses the information you enter to keep your medication journal, track stock and show reminders: medicine names, short names, doses, intake and effect times, personal ratings, notes, refills, schedules and app settings.

Storage and security

Your records and settings are encrypted in the app's private storage. Encryption keys are protected by your device. Medic Logger does not upload, automatically sync or back up your journal. Zerrino cannot recover lost app data.

Collection and sharing

The journal works offline, without an account, advertising, analytics or tracking. Medic Logger does not send your journal, device identifiers or usage history to Zerrino. It does not access HealthKit, Health Connect, your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Notifications and shortcuts

Notifications, Live Activities and home-screen shortcuts can display medicine names and status. Control their visibility in Medic Logger and your device settings. Notification permission is used for medication status and reminders; Android alarm access helps deliver reminders at the chosen time.

Exports

Exports are not encrypted, including ZIP files. You choose where to save or share them. A cloud provider or recipient you select handles that copy under its own policies. Protect exported files that contain health information.

Retention and deletion

Keep records for as long as you choose. Delete doses from Journal, then delete medicines once their doses are removed. Archiving keeps history. Clear app storage on Android or choose Delete App on iOS to remove the local journal; Offload App on iOS retains data. Delete exported files separately.

External links and support

Opening a website gives that site your browser's normal connection information. Ko-fi and its payment providers handle optional donations under their own policies. Medic Logger includes no journal data in these links. If you email support, Zerrino receives your email address and message to respond and keeps them only as needed for that request and applicable legal obligations. Include health information only if you choose to share it.

Policy updates

Changes to data handling will be reflected in the policy included with the app and its public version. The date below identifies this version. Contact Zerrino with privacy questions.

Medical notice

Personal journal

Medic Logger is a personal medication journal, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition.

Medical decisions

Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice and before changing treatment. Schedules are set by you; they are not prescriptions or dosing recommendations. Effectiveness scores reflect your own observations.

Reminder limitations

Reminders can be delayed or missed because of device settings, battery limits or system restrictions. Keep another way to follow your prescribed schedule. For an emergency, contact local emergency services.
